- When a module's issue needs a capability the kernel or platform lacks (a view's range, a read across tables), open issues for that capability now, make them block the module's issue, and keep the module's issue as written; never narrow it, and never record a derived value (a display name) to work around the gap. **When:** the issue's acceptance criteria cannot be met with what the platform offers today. (https://github.com/sunfmin/bizkernel/issues/403)
- When a failure that went red once (a test, a check of the gate) does not fail again, it gets at most two bounded attempts to reproduce it, each leaving its raw output on the issue; still not reproduced, the issue is closed with that record and "If it recurs, reopen with the raw failure output" (the lead closes it with `gh issue close <n> --comment`), or let through to a human, and it is never investigated again. **When:** a flaky failure that no attempt reproduced; one that reproduces is fixed like any other failure. (https://github.com/sunfmin/bizkernel/issues/668#issuecomment-6338325491)
- When a builder's run ended on its own background work (the gate, a suite, a build it started and never read the end of), the builder runs that work again in the foreground, or waits on it with bounded foreground checks, reads what it printed, and declares; nothing else is redone. **When:** a `build` stage failed silent or stopped while its own command was still running.
