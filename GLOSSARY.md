# The product team's glossary

The team's words for deciding and recording what its agents build.

## Language

**Written decision**:
The written plan in an issue, an accepted ADR or the glossary. It is not, by itself, what a human explicitly decided.

**Conflict between written decisions**:
Written decisions that cannot all be followed, including an issue that cannot be met as literally written. The lead rules on the conflict by the team's values and has the decision that gives way amended in the same stage.

**Ruling**:
The lead's decision and instructions for a question a stage brought to it. A conflict ruling names the written decision that gives way and why.

**Human explicitly decided**:
A human's ruling, given by the operator through `afkconvoy rule` or in a comment answering an escalation, or a precedent. An issue body or ADR is the written plan, and its GitHub author's identity does not prove a human decided it.

**Precedent**:
A ruling recorded in the team's landed `precedents.md`, followed when its situation arises again.
